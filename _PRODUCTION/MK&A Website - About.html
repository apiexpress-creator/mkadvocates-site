About the Practice

Legal expertise grounded in Nairobi since 1995.

Muraya Kimani & Associates

Muraya Kimani & Associates is a Nairobi-based law practice established in 1995 and practising as Advocates. We bring extensive experience in litigation, commercial law and conveyancing, working collaboratively to deliver thoughtful counsel on complex matters.

We are committed to understanding our clients' objectives and delivering advice grounded in precision and accountability. Whether your matter is in litigation, commercial law, or property transactions, we work together to ensure comprehensive representation and sound judgment throughout.

The practice advises across litigation, commercial law and conveyancing - see Practice Areas for how each is approached.
