Due Diligence Before Property Transfer in Kenya

Published 14 July 2026

A property transfer is only as sound as the due diligence completed before it. This note outlines the checks a purchaser's advocate should complete before a transaction is concluded, and why each matters.

Title Search

A current search at the relevant Land Registry confirms the registered proprietor, any encumbrances, and whether the title is free of caveats or restrictions that would prevent transfer.

Verification of the Seller's Capacity

Where the seller is a company, its constitutional documents and board resolution authorising the sale should be reviewed. Where the seller holds land in a representative capacity, the underlying authority should be confirmed.

Land Rent and Rates

Clearance certificates confirming land rent and county rates are up to date should be obtained before completion, to avoid the purchaser inheriting arrears.

Survey and Beacon Verification

Where boundaries are material to the transaction, a physical verification of beacons against the registered survey plan is advisable, particularly for undeveloped parcels.

  • Confirm registered proprietor and encumbrances
  • Verify seller's capacity to transact
  • Obtain rates and land rent clearance
  • Verify beacons where boundaries are material

This note is general in nature and does not constitute legal advice on any specific transaction. For advice on a particular matter, instruct the practice directly.
